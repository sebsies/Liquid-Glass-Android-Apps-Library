# Liquid Glass Android Apps Library
Have you ever wanted your favorite FOSS apps to have a somehow... New look? Well, want no further! As I took a few FOSS apps and replaced a few or its UI with Liquid Glass!

# Keep in Mind...
* I made this with **Gemini**. Shocker.
* These apps are **not** made to **mock, make fun of, or disrespect** the creators of these apps. These are simply made to give it Liquid Glass. That's all! These modded versions of these apps **do not** *remove* or *add* any features to themselves. Their differences are just the addition of Liquid Glass.
* These versions of the original apps **may** or **may not** be outdated. It's possible the creators of the apps updated the apps and I never noticed!

# A note to you creators..
Like I said, these apps are made for looks. They are **NOT** made to mock you.
If you creators of these apps, **for any reason**, ask me to take down any of the apps you made that are on this library, I won't hesitate to take them down.
